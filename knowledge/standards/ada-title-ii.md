# Summary

The U.S. Department of Justice adopted a rule requiring state and local government web content to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, with compliance dates that depend on the size of the government entity. Dates and scope have been subject to change.

# Open items

* Re-verify the current compliance dates and any extension or amendment before citing them anywhere.
* Have US counsel review how the rule applies to third-party content, archived content and PDFs.
* This file is guidance for engineering, not legal advice.

# Implication

Treat [WCAG 2.1 AA](/standards/wcag-section-508.md) as non-negotiable for every deployment.
