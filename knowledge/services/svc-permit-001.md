# What it is

Permission from the local government to build, alter or demolish a structure so the work meets local codes.

# Who can use it

Property owners and licensed contractors working on property inside the jurisdiction.

# Steps

1. Confirm the property address and the type of work.
2. Complete the application and upload the required documents.
3. Pay the application fee, if one applies.
4. Staff review the application and may ask for changes.
5. Receive a decision and, if approved, the permit.
6. Schedule required inspections.

# Documents needed

* Site plan or drawings, as the owning department requires.
* Proof of ownership or owner authorization.
* Contractor licence details, where applicable.

# Fees and timing

Set by the owning department. Not stated in this example.
