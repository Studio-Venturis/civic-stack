Illustrative example only. Open-records rules differ by jurisdiction; replace with the real process and have counsel review.

# What it is

A request for copies of records the government holds that are open to the public.

# Who can use it

Anyone. Whether a requester must give a reason or identity is set by local law and is not stated here.

# Steps

1. Describe the records you want, with dates and subjects where possible.
2. Submit the request online, by mail or in person.
3. Receive an acknowledgment with a reference number.
4. Staff locate the records and may ask you to narrow the request.
5. Receive the records, or an explanation of anything withheld.

# Documents needed

None required. A clear description of the records helps.

# Fees and timing

Fees and response times are set by local law and are not stated in this example.

# Help and escalation

Show the records office contact here. Requesters can ask a person to help word a request.

# Not covered

Records about a private individual that are not public, and requests that belong to a court process.
