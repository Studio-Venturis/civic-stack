# Terms

* **Agenda**: the ordered list of items a body will consider at a meeting.
* **Minutes**: the official written record of what happened at a meeting.
* **Ordinance**: a local law adopted by a governing body.
* **Resolution**: a formal expression of a body's decision or intent, often not a law.
* **Commission**: in many counties, the elected governing body (cities often use "council").
* **Public notice**: legally required announcement with rules on timing and publication.
* **Retention class**: how long a record must be kept before disposal.
* **ACR / VPAT**: an accessibility conformance report describing how a product meets accessibility criteria.

Entities these terms attach to are in the [entity model](/domain/municipal-entities.md).
